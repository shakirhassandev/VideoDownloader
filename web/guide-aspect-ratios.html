Understanding video aspect ratios

Updated October 2026 · 5 minute read

An aspect ratio describes the relationship between a video's width and height. It does not describe image quality by itself.

16:9

The standard landscape ratio for most modern video, television and desktop playback.

9:16

A vertical ratio designed for phone-first viewing and short-form vertical video.

1:1

A square format that can work well in feeds where available width is more important than cinematic presentation.

Avoid accidental stretching

When resizing a video, preserve its aspect ratio unless you intentionally want to crop or change the framing. Stretching width or height independently distorts the picture.
