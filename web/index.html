Frequently asked questions

Got questions?

Clear answers about the downloader and browser-based editing tools.

Is Video Downloader Plus free to use?

The core website is designed to work without a paid processing API. Large files still depend on the visitor's device capabilities.

Which websites are supported?

The downloader is intended for direct or authorized media URLs. It does not promise compatibility with every website and should not bypass DRM, authentication or provider restrictions.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for the core tool experience.

Are my local files private?

Compress, Convert and Trim are designed to process files in the browser. Your selected video is not intentionally uploaded to our server by these tools.

Can I download any copyrighted video?

No. Use the downloader only for content you own, public-domain media, openly licensed media where downloading is permitted, or content you are authorized to save.
